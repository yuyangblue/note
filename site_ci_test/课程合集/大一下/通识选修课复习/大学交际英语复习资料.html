    大学交际英语复习资料¶

    整理时间：2026年5月16日

    第一章 重点语法讲解¶

    第一节 时态¶

    英语共有16种时态，以下是考试中最常考的8种：

    一、一般现在时¶

    构成： 主语 + 动词原形（第三人称单数加-s/es）

    用法： | 用法 | 例子 | |------|------| | 习惯性动作 | He drinks coffee every morning. | | 客观真理 | The sun rises in the east. | | 一般事实 | Water boils at 100°C. | | 经常存在的状态 | She is a teacher. |

    二、一般过去时¶

    构成： 主语 + 动词过去式

    用法： | 用法 | 例子 | |------|------| | 过去发生的动作 | I visited Beijing last year. | | 过去存在的状态 | He was very happy then. |

    规则动词过去式变化： - 一般加-ed：work → worked - 以e结尾加-d：love → loved - 辅音+y结尾，y变i加-ed：study → studied - 重读闭音节结尾，双写最后一个辅音加-ed：stop → stopped

    三、一般将来时¶

    构成： will + 动词原形 / be going to + 动词原形

    形式 用法区别 例子
    will 临时决定、预测、承诺 I will help you.
    be going to 计划打算、有迹象 It is going to rain.

    四、现在进行时¶

    构成： be (am/is/are) + 动词-ing

    用法： | 用法 | 例子 | |------|------| | 正在进行的动作 | I am reading a book now. | | 现阶段持续的动作 | She is working on a project these days. |

    五、过去进行时¶

    构成： was/were + 动词-ing

    用法： | 用法 | 例子 | |------|------| | 过去某时刻正在进行的动作 | I was sleeping at 10 pm last night. | | 两个过去的动作同时进行 | He was reading while she was cooking. |

    六、现在完成时¶

    构成： have/has + 过去分词

    用法：

    用法 例子
    过去发生，对现在有影响 I have finished my homework.
    过去开始，持续到现在 I have lived here for 5 years.

    since和for的区别： - since + 时间点：since 2020（自从2020年起） - for + 时间段：for five years（持续五年）

    七、过去完成时¶

    构成： had + 过去分词

    用法： 表示"过去的过去"

    用法 例子
    过去某时刻之前已完成的动作 When I arrived, he had left.
    过去愿望 I wish I had studied harder.

    八、过去将来时¶

    构成： would + 动词原形

    用法： 表示从过去某时刻看将要发生的动作

    例子
    He said he would come tomorrow.

    第二节 语态¶

    一、被动语态的构成¶

    基本公式： 主语 + be + 过去分词

    二、各时态的被动语态形式¶

    时态 主动语态 被动语态
    一般现在时 We clean the room. The room is cleaned.
    一般过去时 She wrote the letter. The letter was written.
    一般将来时 They will finish the work. The work will be finished.
    现在进行时 He is doing the work. The work is being done.
    现在完成时 They have built the bridge. The bridge has been built.

    三、被动语态的使用场景¶

    场景 例子
    不知道动作执行者 The window was broken.
    强调动作承受者 The book is written by this author.
    动作执行者不重要 Paper is made from wood.

    四、被动语态的特殊用法¶

    用法 例子
    get + 过去分词（非正式） He got hurt.
    感官动词+宾语+过去分词 I saw the car damaged.

    第三节 虚拟语气（重点难点）¶

    一、虚拟语气概述¶

    定义： 虚拟语气用于表达与现实相反的假设、愿望、建议或不太可能发生的情况。

    核心原则： 动词形式"时态后退"

    二、if引导的非真实条件句（最重要）¶

    三种情况的对比：

    假设时间 if从句 主句 例子
    与现在事实相反 过去式（be用were） would/could/might + 动词原形 If I were you, I would take the job.
    与过去事实相反 had + 过去分词 would/could/might + have + 过去分词 If she had studied harder, she would have passed.
    与将来事实相反（可能性极小） 过去式 / should + 动词原形 / were to + 动词原形 would/could/might + 动词原形 If it rained tomorrow, we would cancel the trip.

    详细例子解析：

    1. 与现在事实相反：
    2. 现实：我不是你，我没有那么多钱
    3. 虚拟：If I were you, I would accept the offer.（如果我是你，我就会接受这个邀请）
    4. 现实：He doesn't have time.

    5. 虚拟：If he had time, he would help us.（如果他有时间，他会帮我们）

    6. 与过去事实相反：

    7. 现实：你没努力学习，所以你考试没通过
    8. 虚拟：If you had studied harder, you would have passed the exam.
    9. 现实：我没带伞，所以被淋湿了

    10. 虚拟：If I had brought an umbrella, I wouldn't have got wet.

    11. 与将来事实相反：

    12. 现实：明天下雨的可能性很小
    13. 虚拟：If it rained tomorrow, we would stay home.
    14. 现实：中彩票的概率极低
    15. 虚拟：If I were to win the lottery, I would travel around the world.

    三、if倒装的虚拟语气¶

    规则： if从句中有were/had/should时，可以省略if，将这些词提到句首。

    原句 倒装句
    If I were you, I would go. Were I you, I would go.
    If he had known the truth, he would have told me. Had he known the truth, he would have told me.
    If it should rain, we would cancel. Should it rain, we would cancel.

    四、wish后的虚拟语气¶

    表达内容 从句动词形式 例子
    与现在相反 过去式 I wish I were a bird.（实际上我不是）
    与过去相反 had + 过去分词 I wish I had studied harder.（实际上我没努力学习）
    与将来相反 would/could + 动词原形 I wish it would stop raining.（雨还没停）

    五、表示建议、命令、要求的虚拟语气¶

    关键词： - 动词：suggest, advise, recommend, demand, require, request, order, insist - 名词：suggestion, advice, recommendation, demand, requirement, order - 形容词：important, necessary, essential, urgent, strange, natural

    公式： (should) + 动词原形（should可以省略）

    例子：

    句型 例子
    动词后 He suggested that we (should) leave early.
    名词后 It is important that everyone (should) be present.
    形容词后 The teacher required that the homework (should) be handed in.

    六、as if/as though引导的虚拟语气¶

    与什么相反 动词形式 例子
    与现在相反 过去式 He talks as if he knew everything.（实际他不懂）
    与过去相反 had + 过去分词 She looks as if she had cried.（实际她没哭）

    七、含蓄虚拟语气¶

    引导词 例子 对应if句
    without Without water, all living things would die. If there were no water...
    but for But for your help, I would have failed. If you hadn't helped me...
    otherwise I didn't know his number, or I would have called you. If I had known...

    第四节 定语从句¶

    一、关系代词¶

    关系词 先行词 在从句中的作用 例子
    who 人 主语/宾语 The man who helped me is a teacher.
    whom 人 宾语（可省略） The girl (whom) I met is Mary.
    which 物 主语/宾语 The book which I bought is interesting.
    that 人/物 主语/宾语 The person that called you is my brother.
    whose 人/物 定语 The student whose homework is missing is absent.

    二、关系副词¶

    关系词 先行词 在从句中的作用 例子
    when 时间 时间状语 I'll never forget the day when we met.
    where 地点 地点状语 This is the city where I was born.
    why 原因 原因状语 I don't know the reason why he left.

    三、限制性vs非限制性定语从句¶

    类型 逗号 that/which 能否省略 例子
    限制性 无 that/which（可省略） 不能 The book that belongs to me is lost.
    非限制性 有 which（不用that） 可以 Beijing, which is the capital, is beautiful.

    区别： - 限制性：从句是先行词必不可少的修饰语 - 非限制性：从句是对先行词的补充说明

    四、只用that不用which的情况¶

    情况 例子
    先行词为all, everything, nothing等 All that glitters is not gold.
    先行词被the only, the very, the same修饰 He is the very person that I want.
    先行词被序数词或最高级修饰 This is the best film that I have ever seen.
    先行词既有人又有物 They talked about the people and the places that they remembered.

    五、只用which不用that的情况¶

    情况 例子
    非限制性定语从句 My car, which I bought last year, is very comfortable.
    介词后 The room in which we live is large.
    先行词是that Let me show you the letter that came this morning.

    第五节 非谓语动词¶

    一、动词不定式（to do）¶

    用法 例子
    表目的 I came here to study.
    表将来 He seems to be happy.
    表意料之外的结果 He hurried to the station, to find the train had left.
    感官动词后 I saw him cross the street.

    二、动名词（doing）¶

    用法 例子
    作主语 Smoking is bad for health.
    作宾语 I enjoy reading books.
    表主动、正在进行 I remember meeting him before.
    习惯动作 Living in the city costs a lot.

    三、分词（done/doing）¶

    类型 用法 例子
    现在分词（doing） 表主动、正在进行 The sleeping baby is cute.
    过去分词（done） 表被动、已完成 The broken window needs repair.

    四、非谓语动词对比表¶

    形式 主/被动 时间 例子
    to do 主动 将来 I want to help you.
    to be done 被动 将来 The work needs to be done.
    doing 主动 进行/习惯 I enjoy reading.
    done 被动 完成 Broken glass cuts.
    having done 主动 完成 Having finished the work, I left.
    being done 被动 进行 The building being built is our school.

    第六节 倒装句¶

    一、部分倒装¶

    触发词： never, rarely, hardly, seldom, not only, only, little等否定词提前

    原句 倒装句
    I have never seen such a thing. Never have I seen such a thing.
    He didn't realize it until she arrived. Not until she arrived did he realize it.
    Only in this way can you succeed. Only in this way can you succeed.

    二、全部倒装¶

    触发词： here, there, out, in, up等副词 + 动词 + 主语

    例子
    Here comes the bus.
    There lies a beautiful lake.
    Out rushed the children.

    三、虚拟语气倒装¶

    原句 倒装句
    If I had been there, I would have helped. Had I been there, I would have helped.
    If you were to leave, please tell me. Were you to leave, please tell me.

    第五章 阅读理解答题技巧¶

    第一节 题型分类及解题策略¶

    一、主旨大意题¶

    标志词： The main idea / The best title / The passage mainly discusses

    策略： - 看首段和末段 - 看每段首句 - 注意反复出现的关键词

    干扰选项特征： - 过于宽泛 - 过于狭窄 - 与原文无关

    二、细节理解题¶

    标志词： According to the passage / It is stated that / Which of the following is true?

    策略： 1. 定位关键词 2. 回到原文查找 3. 仔细核对选项与原文是否一致 4. 注意同义替换

    三、推理判断题¶

    标志词： It can be inferred / We can conclude / The author implies that

    策略： - 理解字面意思的基础上进行推断 - 注意作者的言外之意 - 排除过度推断的选项

    四、词义猜测题¶

    标志词： The word "..." in the passage means / The underlined part probably means

    策略： - 根据上下文语境推测 - 注意生词周围的定义、解释、例子 - 分析词根词缀
